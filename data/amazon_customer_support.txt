Amazon Customer Support Knowledge Base

RETURNS AND REFUNDS
Most items shipped from Amazon.com can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Some categories, such as electronics, have a 30-day return window that starts from the delivery date, while items sold and shipped by third-party sellers may have different return windows set by the seller. To start a return, go to Your Orders, select the item, and choose "Return or replace items." Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3-5 business days after the returned item is received and processed at the warehouse. If you paid with a gift card, the refund is credited back to your Amazon gift card balance. Items must be returned in their original condition with all accessories and packaging unless they are defective.

ORDER STATUS AND TRACKING
You can check the status of any order by visiting "Your Orders" in your Amazon account. Each order shows an estimated delivery date and a tracking link once the package has shipped. If your package shows as delivered but you cannot find it, wait 24 hours (packages are sometimes marked delivered before they physically arrive), then check with neighbors or your building's front desk. If it still doesn't turn up, contact customer service to report a missing package and request a replacement or refund.

SHIPPING
Amazon offers several shipping speeds: Standard (3-5 business days), Expedited (2 business days), and Prime members get free two-day or same-day shipping on eligible items depending on location. International shipping is available to many countries but delivery times and customs fees vary by destination. Shipping costs and delivery estimates are shown at checkout before you place an order.

AMAZON PRIME
Amazon Prime is a paid membership that includes free fast shipping on eligible items, access to Prime Video, Prime Music, and exclusive deals during events like Prime Day. Prime costs $14.99/month or $139/year in the US. You can cancel Prime at any time from "Manage Prime Membership" in Account Settings; if you cancel within 3 business days of signing up and haven't used any benefits, you're eligible for a full refund.

PAYMENT AND BILLING
Amazon accepts major credit and debit cards, Amazon gift cards, and in some regions, direct debit or "Pay in installments" options. If a charge on your statement doesn't match your order total, it may be a temporary authorization hold that will be adjusted once the order ships — the final charge only occurs when the item ships. If you see a charge you don't recognize, check "Your Orders" and "Your Account > Login & Security" first, then contact customer service if it's still unclear.

ACCOUNT AND PASSWORD
To reset your password, go to the sign-in page and select "Forgot password." Enter the email or phone number associated with your account, and Amazon will send a verification code or reset link. For security, Amazon never asks for your password, full credit card number, or verification codes over phone or email — treat such requests as phishing attempts and report them.

DAMAGED OR DEFECTIVE ITEMS
If an item arrives damaged or defective, you can request a free replacement or a full refund within the standard return window, and in many cases you won't need to return the damaged item. Report the issue as soon as possible via "Your Orders > Problem with order" for the fastest resolution.

CANCELLING AN ORDER
Orders can be cancelled for free as long as they haven't entered the shipping process yet. Go to "Your Orders," select the order, and choose "Cancel items." If the order has already shipped, you'll need to wait for delivery and then start a standard return instead.

CONTACTING CUSTOMER SERVICE
If your issue isn't resolved through self-service options, you can reach Amazon Customer Service through the "Help" section of the website or app, via live chat, or by phone, depending on your region. Have your order number ready to speed up the process.
